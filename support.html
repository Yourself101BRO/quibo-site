How do I cancel my subscription?

In the app: Account → Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Quibo. Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or billing period ends to avoid the next charge.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I switched phones and lost my plan

Open Quibo → Account → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How is “market value” calculated?

It's the typical asking price of comparable Buy It Now listings for the same item and grade, with outliers trimmed. Things often sell for less than they're listed at, so the flip calculator assumes you'd resell at the cheaper end of current listings and includes fees, shipping and sales tax. It's an estimate — use “See recent eBay sales” on any deal to check what it actually sold for.

Why can't I see some deals?

On the free plan, deals show up an hour after Quibo finds them. Quibo Pro members see every deal the moment it's found, plus alerts, Scan and the flip calculator.

A deal was sold or wrong

Deals move fast and listings can change after Quibo sees them. If a listing looked like a mismatch (wrong grade, fake, lot), email us the item link and we'll tighten the filters.
