How do I cancel my subscription?

In the app: Account → Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Quibo. Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or billing period ends to avoid the next charge.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I switched phones and lost my plan

Open Quibo → Account → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How is “market value” calculated?

From comparable Buy It Now listings for the same item and grade, with outliers trimmed and a conservative estimate taken. It's an estimate — use “See recent eBay sales” on any deal to check what it actually sold for.

Why can't I see some deals?

On the free plan, trading card deals show up an hour after Quibo finds them. Paid plans see them right away and differ by category and by the size of flips they show: Quibo shows flips up to $50 profit each, Pro up to $250, Black has no cap. Black also gets a 10-minute head start on new top-scoring deals.

A deal was sold or wrong

Deals move fast and listings can change after Quibo sees them. If a listing looked like a mismatch (wrong grade, fake, lot), email us the item link and we'll tighten the filters.
