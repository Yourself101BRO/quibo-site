How do I cancel my subscription?

In the app: Account → Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Quibo. Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or billing period ends to avoid the next charge.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I switched phones and lost my plan

Open Quibo → Account → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How is “usually sells for” calculated?

Card prices come from JustTCG, which averages real recent sales for each card, condition and printing. Quibo uses the lower of the card's 7-day and 30-day averages, so a card whose price is still falling doesn't look like a bargain. Profit includes sales tax, and the fees and shipping of where you'd resell (or a slightly lower price when you sell in person). It's an estimate: use “Recent sales” on any deal to check what it actually sold for.

Why can't I see some deals?

The free plan shows the day's best 3 deals. Quibo Pro shows every deal that fits your parameters, every day. If you see nothing at all, lower your minimum profit or raise your budget in the feed's parameters.

A deal was sold or wrong

Prices are checked once a day, so a cheap copy can sell before you get to it. Quibo hides prices that look too good to be true (a mislabeled condition, a price far under anything it sold for, a card that's still crashing). If one gets through, email us the card and we'll tighten the filters.
