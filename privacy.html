Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Quibo is built to need as little of your information as possible. There's no Quibo account, no ads, no analytics SDKs and no tracking across apps or websites.

What stays on your device

  • Your onboarding answers (categories, goal, budget), saved deals, alerts and app settings.
  • Saved deals store listing details such as title, price and photos — never the seller's identity.
  • A small copy of your top deals for the Home Screen widget.

This data lives only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it.

What our server receives

  • Deal requests. The app asks our server for the current deal feed. The request contains no personal information.
  • Scan searches. When you use Scan, the text you type is sent to our server to search listings. Results are cached for about 20 minutes and are not linked to you.
  • IP address. Like any web server, ours sees the network address a request comes from. We use it only to prevent abuse (rate limiting), keep it in memory for about an hour, and don't store it or link it to anything else.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple. We never see your name, email or payment details. The app checks your subscription status with Apple on your device.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, deal alerts and trial reminders are created on your device. We don't use a push-notification service or collect a device token.

Links to other sites

Buttons such as “Buy on eBay” and “Check other markets” open eBay, Mercari, Facebook Marketplace and other third-party sites or apps. Those services have their own privacy policies. Some eBay links are affiliate links, which lets eBay know a purchase came from Quibo; eBay may use cookies to do this.

Data sources

Listing data comes from eBay's public APIs. Quibo doesn't collect or store information about eBay buyers or sellers.

Children

Quibo isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can turn off notifications in iOS Settings, delete saved deals and alerts in the app, and remove all app data by deleting the app. Because we don't keep personal data on our servers, there's nothing tied to you for us to export or delete — but you can always contact us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
