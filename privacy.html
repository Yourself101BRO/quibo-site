Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Quibo is built to need as little of your information as possible. There's no Quibo account, no ads, no analytics SDKs and no tracking across apps or websites.

What stays on your device

  • Your onboarding answers (categories, goal, budget), saved deals, alerts and app settings.
  • Saved deals store listing details such as title, price and photos — never the seller's identity.
  • A small copy of your top deals for the Home Screen widget.

This data lives only on your iPhone (except for instant alerts, below). Deleting the app deletes it.

What our server receives

  • Deal requests. The app asks our server for the current deal feed. If you have a plan, the request includes the signed subscription record Apple gives the app (product, transaction number and expiry date), so our server can check it's genuine and show you member deals. It contains no name, email or payment details, and we don't store it or link it to anything else.
  • Card searches. When you check a card, its name and number (typed, or read from the camera on your device) are sent to our server to look up its market price. Prices are cached so repeat checks are fast, and searches are not linked to you.
  • Instant alerts. If you have a plan and allow notifications, the app sends our server your device's push token (a random identifier Apple issues for sending notifications to this app), your alert settings and when your plan renews, so we can notify you the moment a matching deal is found. This isn't linked to your name, email or Apple ID. It's deleted when you cancel your plan or turn notifications off, when Apple tells us the app was removed, or after 30 days without the app checking in.
  • IP address. Like any web server, ours sees the network address a request comes from. We use it only to prevent abuse and to count the free plan's daily card checks, keep it in memory for up to a day, and don't store it or link it to anything else.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple. We never see your name, email or payment details. The app checks your subscription with Apple on your device, and sends Apple's signed subscription record to our server as described above.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, trial reminders are created on your device. Deal alerts are sent by our server through Apple Push Notification service (see "Instant alerts" above), or created on your device when instant alerts aren't available.

Links to other sites

Buttons such as “Buy on TCGplayer”, “Recent sales” and “Get alerts” open TCGplayer, eBay, Mercari, Facebook Marketplace and other third-party sites or apps. Those services have their own privacy policies. Some links may be affiliate links, which lets the marketplace know a purchase came from Quibo; it may use cookies to do this.

Data sources

Card prices come from JustTCG's API. Quibo doesn't collect or store information about buyers or sellers on any marketplace.

Children

Quibo isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can turn off notifications in iOS Settings, delete saved deals and alerts in the app, and remove all app data by deleting the app. Because we don't keep personal data on our servers, there's nothing tied to you for us to export or delete — but you can always contact us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
